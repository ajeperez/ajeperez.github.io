Profile — project leader, builder, inventor

I work at the intersection of project management, regulated product delivery and cross-functional leadership. My background is in microsystems engineering (microtechnique), with a Master's in Innovation for Product & Business Development — and a decade of hands-on project ownership in startups and MedTech.

At Geneva University Hospitals (HUG) I owned the Pneumoscope project from concept to industrial-scale production: scope, schedule, budget, risk, stakeholder alignment and the full EU MDR Class IIa CE certification. Earlier, I co-founded and managed the technical project portfolio of 3Dfunlab, a 3D-printing hardware startup.

What I enjoy most is the rare combination this work demands — turning ambiguity into a structured, delivered project, while keeping it safe, validated and certifiable.
